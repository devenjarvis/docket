Overprint

Docket is printed like a risograph zine: two drums of ink on cheap paper, a little off register. The key drum carries every word and line: what is in the pull request, and what the agent found, which is dashed and set in italic. The spot drum is only ever yours: your actions, your decisions, what waits on you.
