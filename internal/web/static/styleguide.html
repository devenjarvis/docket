Whiteprint

Review is redlining. Three parties mark the same sheet and nobody may confuse them: ink (solid) is what is in the pull request, pencil (dashed, grey) is what the agent did or found, red (solid, heavy) is what the reviewer decides. Line style carries the meaning; colour only confirms it.

Spacing and lines

Everything sits on the 8 px unit --u; components write multiples as calc(var(--u) * n). Corners are square. Nothing has a radius or a shadow.

u-half4px
calc(var(--u) / 2): between a title and its meta line, tight rows of stamps (.gap-s, .mt-s).
u8px
--u, the unit. Gaps inside a control group (.gap-u, .mt).
u-1.512px
Cell padding, gaps inside a block (.block, .gap-m).
u-216px
Gap between items in a row (.row), padding of a pencil box.
u-324px
Padding of the reviewer's panel; the page gutter; between masthead and sheet.
u-432px
Padding of a sheet body; gap between blocks on a sheet (.gap-l).
u-540px
Between sections of the full diff (.gap-xl).
