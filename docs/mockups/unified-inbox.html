C · How it sorts The order is a short list of rules you can read, not a score you have to trust. Every row names the rule that placed it.

Move the fewest minutes that free the most people. Close things before you open new ones.

A pull request you have reviewed but not re-checked, or one of yours that is green but not merged, costs a minute to finish and keeps someone waiting until you do. The inbox clears those first, then gives your judgment to whatever only you can unblock, then the reading nobody waits on.

The rules, in order

  1. Band by the size of the move. Finish: two minutes or less, and it lands or unblocks something: merge, push a held step, re-check what an author changed for you, reply, nudge. Decide: needs your judgment: a review with decisions, or a conflict on yours. Read and sign: nothing to ask.
  2. In a band, who waits on you. It merges on your move (you are the last required approval, or it is yours and ready), then someone waiting more than a working day, then an author who is reviewing one of yours, then the rest.
  3. Then the shortest first, by the agent's estimate of your minutes.
  4. Then the oldest request.

What moves a row

  1. Up to Finish: an author answers your decisions (you re-check only what changed); ana approves yours and it goes green; a review on yours has waited two working days (the move is a nudge, or asking a code owner with fewer open reviews).
  2. Down to Not your move: a draft; your agent is working on it; checks are running; you snoozed it until the author pushes, a check finishes, or tomorrow.
  3. Never under your cursor. New and moved rows are marked and the order settles when you come back to the list. Nothing leaves the list without a reason on it.

Left out on purpose: who the author is, labels like “urgent”, and how big the pull request is in lines. Those are what people sort by when they have nothing better; Docket knows the minutes and who is waiting.

For you to decide

  1. Your own judgment calls against others' reviews. This mockup puts a conflict on yours below a review someone is blocked on, because the agent is already working on yours. The other way gets yours merged sooner.
  2. The budget's default. 30 minutes, then whatever you last chose.
  3. Notifications. Only a new row in Finish notifies, so a ping always means a move of a minute or two.
